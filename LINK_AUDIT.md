## Official links that may block automated checkers

Some recruiting platforms return 403 or otherwise suppress automated page retrieval even when the URL is valid in a normal browser. In this audit, Applied Medical and Lockheed Martin showed that behavior. Keep them as official destinations and verify manually in Chrome/Safari/Edge if the script flags them.

## Important distinction

A link can be technically valid while a specific job is no longer open. Career-home links should be stable; job/evidence links are intentionally time-sensitive. Before the fair or before applying, rerun the checker and manually confirm each specific job posting.
